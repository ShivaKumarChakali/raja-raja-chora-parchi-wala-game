❓ How to play

1. Draw a slip. Every round the paper slips are shuffled. You see only your own role. Only the King is revealed at the start.

2. Follow the chain. King finds Queen → Queen finds Minister → … → Police finds Thief. Tap a player to guess they hold the next role.

3. Wrong guess? You and the player you guessed swap slips. The new holder of your role tries again. Wrong guesses cost points if the penalty is on.

4. Round ends when the Police correctly finds the Thief. Points are for your final role after all swaps. Thief always scores 0.

5. Game: 10 rounds. Highest total wins. A tie at the end means one tie-breaker round.
